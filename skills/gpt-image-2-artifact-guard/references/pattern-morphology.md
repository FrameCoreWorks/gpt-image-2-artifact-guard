# Periodic microtexture: recognition and limits

Use for reported triangular, diagonal checker, diamond, cellular or scale-like repetition. These names describe appearance, not model internals.

## What the target can look like

Fine bright/dark clusters fall into an unexpectedly regular diagonal rhythm. Paired triangular lobes can read as tiny diamonds, scales or a checker lattice within otherwise photographic detail. The effect may organize speckles, highlights or local contrast rather than resemble a flat overlay.

Describe the observed scale relative to the image and depicted objects, direction, affected regions, and whether it follows the material. Do not invent a universal pixel period. Crossing materials or ignoring perspective strengthens suspicion, but is not required: a defect can be confined to one patch of sky, skin or foliage.

Separate these families:

| Appearance | Distinguishing observation |
| --- | --- |
| Periodic microtexture | Small repeated tonal cells within detail, without necessarily breaking object contours |
| Macro tiling/duplication | Large copied or displaced image patches, straight seams, broken contours |
| False microdetail | Implausible pores, brittle specks or worm-like texture, which need not be periodic |
| Designed pattern | Intended weave, halftone, triangle design, scales or lattice consistent with the brief |
| Display artifact | Appearance depends on thumbnail, screenshot, zoom or resampling; original not yet checked |

A request to illustrate these defects, including a repository banner, deliberately wants the pattern. Do not cleanse its subject or count the illustration as a naturally occurring failure.

## Avoid false positives

- Water droplets, sparks, spray and particles can genuinely form dense bright clusters. Look for suspicious repeated structure; do not classify every bright dot as corruption.
- A wicker basket or gingham fabric is intentionally repetitive. Check form, material and perspective.
- Natural pores and freckles vary with age, lighting, makeup and scale. Neither smooth skin nor visible pores prove correctness.
- A background checker may be a transparency preview. A screenshot cannot establish that it is baked into the file.
- A magnified thumbnail cannot settle pixel-level morphology. State the inspected scope and request the original if needed.

Do not claim that either of the user's example images proves a particular backend, watermark or decoder mechanism. Provenance and generation history are separate evidence.

## Preflight and recovery

Keep the scene's required complexity. Describe legitimate variation and material-following detail first. If a pattern is reported or observed, add a narrow exclusion for that surface. For example: preserve leaf veins and irregular highlights while avoiding a repeated diagonal light/dark micro-lattice on the foliage.

This wording is an unbenchmarked hypothesis, not a reliable fix. If the source is already heavily contaminated, discuss whether an authorized local edit or cleaner reference is appropriate. Do not feed a corrupted output back as the style authority by default.

Accept only if the unwanted structure is sufficiently reduced at the intended viewing size and required detail remains. Record unresolved or uncertain cases; do not diagnose from the word "tiling" alone.
